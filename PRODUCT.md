# Zarbafini

A small, calm pre-order shop for Volume I: Sanctuary. Six sets, ten of each, sixty numbered places. Footer reads "Zarbafini, by Zafirenzo"; nothing says Zenith.

- Visitor: arrives from Instagram or WhatsApp on a mid-range phone. Must understand the name in ten seconds, pick a set and size, and pay in under a minute.
- Mode: Persuade (home, story, collection), Operate (set page, checkout), Read (policies, Register).
- Success: 30 paid sets and ~50 waitlist names by 24 October.
- Rule: no set is made until 30 are paid for; otherwise everyone is refunded in full.
- Honesty: the gold is zari border tape, real metallic weave, not handwoven zarbaft. Always say so.
- Avoid: gradients, glitter, gold text on ivory, stock photos, Zenith navy.

Assumptions (from the layout brief, not an interview): set names other than The Honour, prices, garment lines, size measurements, exchange rule, delivery window and seller details are placeholders in `lib/sets.ts` and `lib/site.ts`.
